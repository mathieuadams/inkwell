Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to turn your notes into text, translate them and bill you. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use your notes to train AI models.

1. Who we are

Inkwell ("we", "us") provides the Inkwell website and service. This policy explains what we collect when you use Inkwell, why, and your choices. Questions: info@frontdesklocal.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a hashed password, managed by Amazon Cognito.
  • Your content: the photos and PDFs you upload, the text we extract, your edits, and translations.
  • Usage information: how many pages and translations you've used each month, and basic technical logs (such as request times, errors and IP address) used to run and secure the Service.
  • Billing information: your plan, subscription status, top-up pages bought and a Stripe customer ID. Card details are entered on Stripe's checkout page and are never stored by us.

We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. The app stores your sign-in session in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

3. How we use it

  • To read your handwriting, show you the text, translate it and save it (depending on your plan).
  • To manage your account, subscription, page limits and billing.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To send you service emails, such as verification codes, receipts and important changes.

We don't sell or share your personal information for advertising, and we don't use your content to train AI models.

4. Service providers

We use these providers to run Inkwell. They process data on our behalf, under their own security and privacy commitments:

ProviderPurpose
Amazon Web Services (US)Hosting, storage (S3), sign-in (Cognito), and text recognition and translation through Amazon Bedrock, using Anthropic Claude models. Under Amazon Bedrock's terms, your inputs and outputs are not used to train the models and are not shared with the model provider.
StripePayments, subscriptions, invoices and tax.
Google FontsServes the website's fonts. Your browser requests the fonts from Google, which receives your IP address.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

5. How long we keep your data

PlanPhotos and notes
Free and StarterNot saved. The photo is deleted as soon as the text is read, and the text exists only in your browser until you leave the page.
PlusDeleted automatically 30 days after they were last changed.
ProKept while your subscription is active.
  • After a downgrade or cancellation, saved notes are kept for 30 more days, then the new plan's rules apply.
  • Your email, plan and usage counts are kept while your account exists.
  • Billing records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Technical logs are kept for about 30 days.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Each account can only access its own files, and upload links expire after a few minutes. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you as the law requires if a breach affects you.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access, copy, correct or delete: you can edit and delete notes in the app. To get a copy of your data or delete your account, email info@frontdesklocal.com. We'll respond within 30 days.
  • California residents (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We don't sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.
  • EEA/UK residents (GDPR): we process your data to provide the Service you signed up for (contract), to keep it secure (legitimate interests), and to meet legal duties. You may also object, restrict processing, and complain to your local data protection authority. Your data is stored in the United States.

8. Children

Inkwell isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes

We'll post updates here and change the effective date. If a change is material, we'll tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
