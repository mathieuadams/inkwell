Questions

Can it read old-fashioned cursive?

Usually, yes, including the looping hands common in 19th and early 20th century letters. Very faded ink, heavy crossing-out or shorthand can defeat it; those spots are marked so you know where to look at the original.

What if it gets a word wrong?

Edit it. The text sits in an editor next to the photo, so you can compare and fix names, places and dates before translating or saving.

Which languages can it translate into?

Any common language, in either direction. Seven are one tap away and more are in the dropdown.

Do I have to type anything?

No. Photograph the page and the text appears. Typing is only for corrections.

What happens to my photos?

On the free and Starter plans, the photo is deleted as soon as the page is read and nothing is stored. On Plus it's kept 30 days, on Pro for as long as you subscribe. You can delete anything yourself at any time.

Can I do a whole box at once?

Up to 20 pages per batch, as many batches as your plan allows. Download the whole batch as one text file when it's done.
